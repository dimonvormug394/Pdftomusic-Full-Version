## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **OCR Technology** — Utilizes Optical Character Recognition to read and play music scores directly from PDF files.
- **Text-to-Speech Functionality** — Listen to how songs are sung, enhancing your understanding of the musical pieces.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Intuitive design allows for easy navigation and quick access to all features.
- **Audio Preview** — Hear the score before playing it on your instrument, making practice sessions more effective.
- **Compatibility** — Works seamlessly with various PDF score formats, ensuring broad usability.

### Advanced Features:
- **Adjustable Playback Speed** — Customize the tempo to suit your practice needs.
- **Export Options** — Save your audio files for sharing or further editing.
